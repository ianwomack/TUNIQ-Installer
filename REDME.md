# TUNIQ: tester guide

TUNIQ tunes a lead vocal. Drop in a mono vocal (wav or mp3), pick the key, and it tunes the take; you fix what you
hear. You're testing it before the wider rollout, so tell us everything: what's confusing, what breaks, what you wish it did.

You need a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer) and an internet connection the first time you open it.

## Install

1. Download **TUNIQ-…-arm64.dmg** from the link you were sent and open it.
2. Drag **TUNIQ** onto **Applications**.
3. Open TUNIQ from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app for malware. That's expected:
   this test version isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate.
   - Click **Done**, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the line about TUNIQ and click
     **Open Anyway**, then type your Mac password.
   - On older macOS, right-click TUNIQ and choose **Open** instead.
4. TUNIQ sets itself up (a few minutes, once). Keep the window open.

You only do steps 3 and 4 once. Later updates open straight away.

## Send feedback

Click the speech-bubble button at the top right (next to the gear), or choose **TUNIQ > Send Feedback…**.
Put your name, pick Bug, Idea or Other, and say what happened. Leave **Attach my session** ticked when a song is open:
that sends your edits and settings (not your audio), so we can see exactly what you saw.

Also **Save Session** (⌘S) on songs where something went wrong, and keep the file in case we ask for it.

## Update

When a new version is ready a bar appears at the top: click **Update**. A disk image opens: drag TUNIQ onto
Applications again and choose **Replace**. You can also check any time with **TUNIQ > Check for Updates…**.

## Uninstall

**TUNIQ > Uninstall TUNIQ…** moves the app and its working files (about 1 GB) to the Trash. Your saved sessions and audio
aren't touched. If TUNIQ won't open, double-click **Uninstall TUNIQ** in the disk image, or drag TUNIQ from Applications to the Trash.
